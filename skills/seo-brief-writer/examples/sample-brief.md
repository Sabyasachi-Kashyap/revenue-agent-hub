# SEO Brief: best budget laptop for students

**Audience:** College students and first-year learners buying a laptop for coursework on a limited budget (roughly under ₹50,000 or the local equivalent).
**Search intent:** Comparison. Readers want a short list of options that fit a budget, with reasons to pick each one. Some will be ready to buy.
**Tone and length:** Plain and friendly, about 1,400 words
**Recommended angle:** "What to prioritize at each budget," because it answers the real question (what matters most for coursework) before naming models.
**Alternatives considered:** A single top-five list (too generic); a brand-by-brand review (too narrow for the keyword).

## Meta
- **Title (under 60 characters):** Best Budget Laptops for Students (What to Prioritize)
- **Description (under 155 characters):** Choosing a budget laptop for college? Learn which specs matter for coursework, what to skip, and which models fit different budgets.

## Internal links

## Must include
- If affiliate links are used, add a clear disclosure near the top.

## Verify before publishing
- **VERIFY:** Current prices for every model listed, including the date checked.
- **VERIFY:** Battery and weight figures, taken from manufacturer pages.
- **VERIFY:** Whether any model listed has been discontinued.

## Writer notes
Avoid naming a single "best" model. Give a reason for each pick and say who it suits.
